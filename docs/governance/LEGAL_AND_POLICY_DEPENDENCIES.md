## Source-backed operational constraints

The [official first-time instructions for citizens aged 16 and over](https://homelandsecurity.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/First-Time-Passport-Instructions-Adult-16-2026.pdf) state that applicants must appear in person for their scheduled appointment and list document and TrackPak requirements. The [adult passport application form](https://homelandsecurity.gov.tt/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/PASSPORT-APPL-FORM-ADULT-12-inch-PDF.pdf) includes declarations, a guardian-permission section for applicants under 18, recommender details, citizenship basis, prior passport/travel-document question, references, and officer-only fields. The documents are evidence for mapping; they do not approve a digital substitute.

## Gate before affected implementation

For any affected package, record the approving authority, policy or legal reference, effective date, approved test evidence, and rollback action before implementing a legal equivalence, production integration, biometric workflow, decision authority, payment rule, or custody process. If the required decision is unavailable, keep that implementation slice blocked and continue unrelated Wappler-native work only.
